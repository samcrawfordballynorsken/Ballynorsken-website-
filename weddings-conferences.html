A New Chapter

Gatherings that belong to the wilderness

We're expanding Ballynorsken into a full-service destination for the moments people choose to remember — weddings, retreats and conferences held on an estate built around nature, heritage and hospitality. Whether it's vows exchanged among the pines or a team brought together beneath the northern sky, every gathering here will carry the same authenticity as everything else we do.

Vows in the wild

Intimate ceremonies set among the forest and cabins, with the estate's log-built architecture as a natural backdrop for the day.

Conferences & Retreats

Celebrations

Anniversaries & milestones

Why Ballynorsken

The same promise, extended

Everything we've built here — the log cabins, the café, the country store, the wilderness experiences — was designed with hospitality at its heart. Weddings and conferences are a natural next step: bringing people together on an estate that already knows how to welcome them.
