Our Story

About Ballynorsken

Founded by Sam & Jackie Crawford, Ballynorsken grew from a single kennel into a small estate built around one idea: the Nordic wilderness is worth slowing down for.

The Meaning of Ballynorsken

The Village of the Northern Lights

Ballynorsken is a name born from two worlds. "Bally" comes from the Irish word "Baile," meaning "the village of" or "the town of" — a word found throughout Ireland's place names. "Norsken" is inspired by the Swedish word for "the Norse ones," a natural nod to where we found belonging and a deep sense of connection.

Together, Ballynorsken means the Village of the Northern Lights — a place where a name is our promise, and every experience is rooted in authenticity.

A Place Between Two Worlds

Irish Roots. From the green hills of Ireland comes our heritage of warmth, storytelling and a deep connection to land and community.

Nordic Home. In the far north of Sweden, we found our wilderness — the forests, mountains, clear skies and the Northern Lights that inspired our name.

Our Story

A dream rooted in the North

Ballynorsken was born from a deep love for the Nordic wilderness, our dogs and the land. The journey began with Norwegian Elk Hounds and a passion for tradition and the bond between dog and human — and from that foundation, our dream expanded to everything that embodies the spirit of the north: cabins, culture, training and unforgettable adventures. This is our story. This is our life. This is Ballynorsken.

Our Heritage. For generations, these dogs have worked alongside people in forests and mountains — their courage, intelligence and endurance handed down. We're honoured to continue that tradition and preserve it for the breed's future.

Our Home. We're in the far north of Sweden — deep forests, clear lakes and long winters — between Överkalix and Kalix in Norrbotten. This is where we raise our dogs, build our cabins, welcome guests and live our everyday life. It shapes everything we do.

At the heart of it stands Klippets Tirhild — Elsa — whose image has become the visual ambassador for Ballynorsken.
