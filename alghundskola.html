Älghundskola

Our moose dog school teaches the traditional Nordic discipline of moose hunting with dogs — the bond, the fieldcraft, and the patience it takes to work well together.

Our Purpose

Honouring instinct. Building partnership.

At Ballynorsken Älghundskola, we specialise in training Norwegian Elkhounds (älghundar) to be confident, skilled and reliable partners in the northern wilderness. Our training is rooted in tradition and guided by respect — for the dog, the hunt, and the land we all share.

"A well-trained elkhound doesn't just chase game — it thinks, it listens, and it works with heart."

Our Training Philosophy

Balance. Instinct. Discipline.

Build on Instinct

We encourage natural ability and channel it with guidance, patience and consistency.

Positive Methods

Reward-based training builds trust, confidence and a strong bond between dog and handler.

Real World Experience

Our dogs train in real terrain with real challenges — forest, wildlife and weather.

Handler Education

We teach handlers how to lead, understand and work as a team with their dog.

Lifelong Learning

Training never stops. Every day in the wild is a new lesson.

The Elkhound in the Wild

Born to hunt. Bred to think.

The Norwegian Elkhound is more than a hunting dog — it is a thinking, feeling partner with thousands of years of heritage. Our training develops their natural gifts.

🐾 Exceptional nose and tracking ability

🤝 Independence with cooperation

💪 Courage and determination

🏔️ Endurance and adaptiveness

🏡 Loyalty to handler and home

When instinct and training come together, extraordinary things happen.

Handler. Dog. Team.

Trust

Understanding

Our Commitment

To the dogs. To the land. To the future.

We are committed to preserving the traditions of ethical hunting and responsible dog training. We protect our forests, respect our wildlife and invest in the future of the breed.

"At Ballynorsken Älghundskola, we don't just train dogs — we build legacies."
