Our Dogs, Our Heritage

Norwegian Elk Hounds

A working kennel rooted in one of Scandinavia's oldest breeds — bred for the hunt, at home by the fire, and at the heart of everything Ballynorsken stands for.

An Ancient Heritage

One of the world's oldest spitz breeds

The Norwegian Elkhound has a history stretching back thousands of years in the rugged Nordic landscapes. Bred to hunt elg (moose) in dense forest and harsh terrain, these dogs were valued for their courage, endurance and determination.

They hunted independently, using their keen senses, intelligence and voice to hold the moose at bay until the hunter arrived — a partnership between dog and human built on trust, respect and teamwork.

Our Kennel

Rooted in heritage, built for the future

Ballynorsken Norwegian Elk Hounds was founded on a deep respect for the ancient heritage of the Norsk Elghund Grå — a breed shaped by the wild and trusted by generations. We are a small, dedicated kennel in the heart of northern Sweden, committed to preserving the true working spirit, soundness and beauty of this remarkable breed.

Health First

We breed only from dogs with excellent hip scores, strong conformation and soundness.

Hunting Instinct

Our dogs are born to hunt. We value natural ability, drive and perseverance.

Breed Standard

We breed to the FCI standard, with correct structure, movement and type.

Temperament

Stable, confident and friendly — Elkhounds that are a joy to live and work with.

Longevity
