The Ballynorsken Café

Simple, seasonal food in a warm timber room overlooking the lake — open to guests and visitors alike.

Our Café, Your Place

Where stories are shared and memories are made

At Ballynorsken Café, we believe the best moments in life are the simple ones — a hot coffee, good food, and time spent with friends, family, and the people who matter. Whether you're staying with us, training your dog, exploring the wilderness, or just passing by, you're always welcome here.

Our Philosophy

Authentic Hospitality

Quality Ingredients

Made with Care

Rooted in Nature

Connection & Community

A Warm & Welcoming Atmosphere

Relax. Unwind. Feel at home.

Step inside and feel the warmth of wood, the glow of the fire, and the scent of good coffee. Our café is designed to be a place where you can slow down, enjoy the view, and savour the moment. Inside or out — every seat has a story to tell.
